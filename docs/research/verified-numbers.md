## Decoherence timescales (plate 04 — boundary)

Schlosshauer, *Quantum decoherence*, Physics Reports 831 (2019) 1–57, Table 1
(p. 22), collisional decoherence times in **seconds** for a spatial
superposition of separation Δx = the object's own size:

| Environment | Dust grain (10⁻³ cm) | Large molecule (10⁻⁶ cm) |
|---|---|---|
| Cosmic background radiation | 1 | 10²⁴ |
| Photons at room temperature | 10⁻¹⁸ | 10⁶ |
| Best laboratory vacuum | 10⁻¹⁴ | 10⁻² |
| Air at normal pressure | **10⁻³¹** | 10⁻¹⁹ |

- Plate 04's readout `DUST GRAIN: 10⁻³¹ s IN AIR` ✓ matches row 4.
- `C₆₀ MOLECULE: MILLISECONDS, IN HIGH VACUUM` ✓ consistent with the
  large-molecule / best-vacuum entry (10⁻² s); fullerene Talbot–Lau
  decoherence experiments (Hornberger et al. 2003) agree.
- Zurek, Physics Today 44:10 (1991) p. 36, gives the macroscopic anchor: a
  1 g object, Δx = 1 cm at 300 K has τ_D/τ_R ≈ 10⁻⁴⁰ — even with a
  relaxation time the age of the universe (10¹⁷ s), coherence dies in
  ~10⁻²³ s. (Extracted from the article PDF, p. 7 of the scan.)
- Largest object superposed to date: functionalized oligoporphyrins
  > 25,000 amu — Fein et al., Nature Physics 15, 1242 (2019).

## Bell / entanglement statistics (plates 02 and 10)

Singlet pair measured with detectors set at angles differing by Δθ
(spin-½ convention):

- P(outcomes **same**) = sin²(Δθ/2); P(opposite) = cos²(Δθ/2).
- Δθ = 0° → same 0% (perfect anticorrelation — the act-1 fact).
- Δθ = 60° → same **25%** · Δθ = 120° → same **75%**.

Wigner–d'Espagnat inequality (local hidden variables + the act-1 perfect
anticorrelation ⇒ each pair carries a full answer sheet f with the partner
carrying ¬f; "same" at settings (a,b) ⟺ f(a) ≠ f(b), and Hamming
disagreement obeys the triangle inequality):

    P_same(0°,120°) ≤ P_same(0°,60°) + P_same(60°,120°)

Any local script: 75% ≤ 25% + 25% = 50% — **false**. That single line is the
whole of Bell, in the form the plate can tally live. (Wigner 1970; d'Espagnat,
Scientific American 1979; same structure as the MinutePhysics Venn argument.)

CHSH form used by plate 10: |S| ≤ 2 for any local model; QM reaches
2√2 ≈ 2.83 (Tsirelson bound); game win rates 75% classical vs
cos²(π/8) ≈ 85.4% quantum. ✓ as drawn.

## Page curve (plate 06 — information)

- Hole entropy S_BH ∝ M²; Schwarzschild evaporation M(t) = M₀(1 − t/τ)^⅓,
  so S_BH(t) = S₀ (1 − t/τ)^⅔.
- Thermal Hawking radiation carries entropy ≈ 1.5 × the hole entropy it
  spends (Zurek, PRL 49, 1683 (1982)): S_rad(t) ≈ 1.5 · S₀ (1 − (1 − t/τ)^⅔).
- Hawking's books: S_rad rises monotonically to ≈ 1.5 S₀ at t = τ — the
  radiation never purifies; the record is gone.
- Unitarity's books (Page, PRL 71, 3743 (1993)): S_rad can never exceed the
  entropy of what it's entangled with, S_BH. So the true curve is
  min(S_rad, S_BH): rises, turns over, returns to zero.
- Turnover (**Page time**): 1.5(1 − s) = s with s = (1−u)^⅔ → s = 0.6,
  u = 1 − 0.6^{3/2} ≈ **0.535** of the lifetime — "just past halfway".
  Literature quotes t_Page ≈ 0.5–0.54 τ; the plate draws 0.54.
- Replica-wormhole derivations of the turnover: Penington 2019;
  Almheiri et al. 2019 (the "island" calculations named in the copy).
